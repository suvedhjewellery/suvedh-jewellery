RETURN AND REFUND POLICY

At SUVEDH, customer satisfaction is important to us. To protect product quality
and hygiene, all returned jewellery must meet the conditions described below.

Return Request Period

A return request must be submitted within 7 days from the date the order is
delivered.

Eligible Return Reasons

A return request may be considered when:

- A damaged product was received
- An incorrect product was delivered
- The product has a verified manufacturing defect
- An applicable size-related issue has been reported

Mandatory Unboxing Video

For damaged, incorrect or missing-product claims, a continuous and unedited
unboxing video is mandatory. The video must clearly show:

- The sealed parcel
- The shipping label
- The opening of the package
- The complete product and its condition

Product Condition

A returned product must be:

- Completely unused and unworn
- Free from scratches, stains, perfume, makeup or other signs of use
- Returned with all original tags intact
- Returned in its original packaging
- Returned with the invoice, certificates and included accessories
- Packed safely to avoid damage during return shipping

Used, worn, altered or damaged products will not be accepted.

Return Inspection

After the returned product reaches SUVEDH, our team will inspect:

- The condition of the product
- Original tags and packaging
- Signs of use, alteration or damage
- Whether the returned product matches the original order
- Whether the return meets the conditions in this policy

A return is not automatically approved merely because the product was sent
back. Approval is subject to inspection.

Refund Processing

Once the returned product passes inspection, SUVEDH will initiate the refund
within 45 working days.

The time required for the amount to appear in the customer's account may depend
on the bank, UPI provider or payment gateway.

Where possible, refunds will be made to the original payment method.

Non-Returnable Cases

A return may be rejected when:

- The item has been used or worn
- The item contains scratches, stains or other signs of use
- Original tags, packaging or accessories are missing
- The product was damaged after delivery
- The return request was submitted after the stated return period
- A required unboxing video was not provided
- The returned item does not match the original order
- The issue relates to normal wear and tear
- The product falls under a hygiene-sensitive or non-returnable category

How to Request a Return

Contact SUVEDH and provide:

- Order number
- Customer name
- Reason for return
- Product photographs
- Unboxing video where required
